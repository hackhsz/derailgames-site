Derail Games Ltd

We build measurement tools for the Roblox economy.

Roblox is a market with tens of thousands of competing titles and almost no public instrumentation. We build the instruments — live counts, honest forecasts, and the record of whether those forecasts landed.

What we build

One product today. It is the whole company.

BloxTrace

Concurrency intelligence for Roblox experiences

bloxtrace.com →

BloxTrace tracks live concurrent-player counts across roughly 99,000 Roblox experiences, and forecasts where a game’s audience is heading. On the web, with an iOS app in development, alongside commissioned research on individual titles.

How we work

Measured, not asserted

Every number we publish comes from observed behaviour — concurrency, retention, how comparable titles moved. We do not run surveys, and we do not describe what players said.

Forecasts carry error bars

A projection without a stated corridor is an opinion. We publish the corridor with the call, and we publish the score afterwards whether it flatters us or not.

Small and technical

A one-person company with working software, not a deck. The pipeline, the site and the app are built and run in-house.
