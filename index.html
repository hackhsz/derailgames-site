We find what players want before anyone else, and make it.

AI can now make almost any game. It can’t tell you which one people will actually play. We measure that, then make original games, characters and music for the audiences nobody is serving yet — starting on Roblox.

What we do

Find

We measure what people actually play. Our own instrument follows live player counts across tens of thousands of Roblox games, and shows us where demand is large and supply is thin.

Make

We build original games for those players, quickly. AI does the expensive, repetitive work; people do everything players can feel.

Keep

We own the characters, worlds and music we create, and take the ones players love beyond the game.

BloxTrace

Live player counts and forecasts for Roblox

bloxtrace.com →

The instrument behind our decisions, open to everyone. BloxTrace tracks live player counts across tens of thousands of Roblox games and publishes forecasts with a stated range, scored afterwards. On the web, with an iOS app in development.

How we work

Measured, not guessed

Every decision starts from observed behaviour — what people play, come back to and pay for. We don’t run surveys, and we don’t build on hunches.

Forecasts carry error bars

A projection without a stated range is an opinion. We publish the range with the call, and the score afterwards, whether it flatters us or not.

Small and technical

A small team from across the games industry. Our tools, pipeline and games are built and run in-house.
